By Emma Bolton and Ineke Knot Number of Samples Collected: 60 National Park: Beinn Eighe NNR + Scottish Crown Estates Environments of focus: Coastal samples, terrestrial soil. With Scotland living up to its weather reputation, the planning of our permit areas were in a state of flux as we scoped out three different areas of the Scottish highlands to sample over the 7 day expedition. With the weather window for sampling the Isle of Skye looking the best earlier in the week, we headed across the bridge from the mainland to sample the most Northern coast of Skye.

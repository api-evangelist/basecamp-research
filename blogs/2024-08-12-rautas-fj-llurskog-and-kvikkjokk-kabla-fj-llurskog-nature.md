By Marlon Clark The environment around Rautas and Kvikkjokk-Kabla nature reserves are characterised by a subarctic climate, with long, cold winters and short, mild summers. The landscape is dominated by mountains, forests, lakes, fens, and bogs. The region’s cold and often harsh climate makes it an ideal location for collecting samples existing on the envelope of survivability.

Locations : Sao Miguel & Pico National Parks By Emma Bolton The Azores Archipelago, with its sixteen major central volcanoes, twelve of which are silicic and crowned with calderas, and eight hiding beneath the ocean’s surface, presents a compelling case for biodiscovery. This geological wonderland includes eleven volcanic ridges , nine polygenetic volcanoes and seven active ridges. The underwater realm is equally intriguing, featuring offshore deep-sea hydrothermal fields and seamount volcanoes..

By Emma Bolton Pirin National Park is located in the Pirin Mountains in southwestern Bulgaria. It is renowned for its rugged landscapes, with more than 70 glacial lakes, towering peaks, and dense forests. The environment here is predominantly alpine with some mountainous terrain (including Vihren, which is the second highest peak in Bulgaria).
